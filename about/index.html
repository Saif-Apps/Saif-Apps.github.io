What CleanLens is built to do

CleanLens helps surface exact duplicate photos, similar shots, screenshots, large videos, and other cleanup opportunities. The app is designed around on-device analysis and a review-first flow so you remain responsible for what moves toward deletion.

Privacy and control

CleanLens does not require an account to analyze your photo library. Photo analysis is designed to stay on your iPhone, and selected items remain part of a review flow before the normal iOS deletion confirmation.
